Does a high score mean a website is safe?＋

No score can guarantee a website is safe. A high score only means our selected checks found few warning signals. Sellers and websites can change, and some harmful behavior cannot be detected by an automated scan.

What does a “missing” signal mean?＋

It means we couldn’t find that information in the page we could access. It may exist elsewhere or be temporarily unavailable. Missing information is not proof of fraud.

Are community reports verified?＋

Reports start as user-submitted claims. They are labeled with their current review status. A report is not treated as verified unless a moderator records a decision based on the available evidence.

Do you send information to the website?＋

No. TrustShield makes a limited read-only request to retrieve a public page. It won’t log in, place an order, or submit any forms on the site.
